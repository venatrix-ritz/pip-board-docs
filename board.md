# The Board

Everything a rule matched lands on the Board. It's the app's main screen and the reason it's called
ProBoard rather than a notification filter.

---

## Board vs timeline

Two views over the same data.

**Board** shows what's still live — anything not yet dealt with. This is the working surface: what
needs your attention right now.

**Timeline** shows everything captured today, dismissed or not, in the order it arrived. It resets
at midnight. Dismissing something removes it from the Board but never from the timeline, so the
timeline stays an honest record of what actually came through.

---

## Cards

Each entry shows the app, the notification's title and text, when it arrived, and which rule caught
it. If the rule used branches, the matching branch name appears too — so you can tell at a glance
*why* something is on your board, not just that it is.

Entries from the same notification thread collapse into one active card. A new message in a
conversation replaces the previous card rather than stacking, so an active thread doesn't bury
everything else. The timeline still keeps each individual message.

---

## Pinning

A pinned entry is one with a **persistent** reminder attached. It stays on the Board until you
clear it inside ProBoard.

The important part: clearing the underlying notification from the system shade doesn't clear the
pin. Ordinary notifications vanish the moment you swipe them away, whether or not you actually
dealt with them. A pin doesn't — it comes back, on its schedule, until you act on it in the app.

Anything not pinned behaves normally: dismiss the source notification and it leaves the Board too.

---

## Clearing

Individual entries clear with a swipe or the ✕ on the card. **Clear all** empties the Board in one
go.

Clearing cancels any reminders attached to those entries, so a cleared item won't come back later.
Nothing is deleted from the timeline.

---

## Widget

The **Alerts** widget mirrors the Board to your home screen — live, without opening the app.

It lists active entries and shows a count, tapping an entry opens the app, and it carries its own
clear-all button which cancels reminders exactly like clearing inside the app does.

The widget follows your theme colour and updates whenever the Board changes.
